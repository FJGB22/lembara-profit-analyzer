Lembara Profit Analyzer

by Jonathan Kuniardi · Lembara Studio

Small handmade shops often know their sales but not their real profit per product. This tool combines a shop's orders with its material and recipe costs to show:

  • net profit after platform fees, materials, shipping and discounts
  • profit per product and per unit, and which orders lost money
  • what needs restocking

It reads Etsy orders through Etsy's official API with read-only, seller-approved access, or from Etsy and Shopify CSV exports.
