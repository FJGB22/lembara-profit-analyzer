Privacy Policy – Lembara Profit Analyzer

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Lembara Profit Analyzer is a reporting tool operated by Jonathan Kuniardi (Lembara Studio). It helps a shop owner see real profit per product after platform fees, material costs, shipping and discounts. It is used only for sellers who ask for it and who connect their own shop.

What data is accessed

With the seller's permission, given through Etsy's official sign-in (OAuth), the tool reads the seller's shop receipts using read-only access (transactions_r, shops_r). From each order it keeps only: order number, order date, item title, SKU, quantity, item price, shipping charged, discount and order status.

Etsy's order data also contains buyer names and shipping addresses. The tool does not keep, store or use buyer names, addresses or email addresses.

The seller may also provide their own product, material and recipe costs. These are combined with the order data only for that seller's report, at the seller's request.

How data is used

  • Only to calculate the seller's own profit, fee, cost and restock reports.
  • Never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or marketing.
  • Never combined with data from other shops or other sources about the seller or their buyers.

Optional AI summary

If the seller turns on the optional plain-English summary, the calculated totals and per-product figures (not buyer information) are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the summary. This feature is off unless the seller asks for it.

Storage and retention

Data is processed on the operator's computer or in a spreadsheet owned by the seller. Access tokens are stored locally in a private file and are never published. Order data and tokens are kept only as long as needed to provide the reports the seller asked for, and are deleted when the seller ends the service or asks for deletion.

Your choices

A seller can revoke the tool's access at any time from their Etsy account settings, and can ask for all stored data to be deleted by contacting the address below.
